# Building Detection with GeoAI in Matungu, Kenya

**Status:** In progress

## Problem
Many rural and fast-growing areas are poorly mapped. Can a deep
learning model detect buildings from open aerial imagery of Matungu,
and how does it compare with Google Open Buildings and OSM?

## Objectives
1. Complete the GeoAI book's segmentation chapters.
2. Train a model with an IoU of 0.5 or higher.
3. Compare results with Google Open Buildings and OSM, and feed the
   best layer into the healthcare accessibility project.
